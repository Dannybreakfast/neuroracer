NeuroRacer Trainer

Focus & multitasking training

Inspired by NeuroRacer (Anguera, Gazzaley et al., Nature 2013). This is an independent hobby trainer — not the clinical product (EndeavorRx/Akili) and makes no medical claims.

How to play

Drive: keep the car centered in the lane as the road winds. Keyboard ←/→ or A/D; touch ◀ ▶.

Signs: shapes flash briefly. Respond only to the target () with Space or TAP. Ignore everything else.

Multitask does both. Difficulty adapts every ~20 s per task (staircase aiming at ~80% success): road speed/curvature and sign display time tighten when you do well and ease when you don't.

Baseline runs short sign-only, drive-only and multitask blocks to measure your multitasking cost.
